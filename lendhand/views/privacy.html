Who we are

What we collect

When you register, we ask for:

  • your full name, mobile / WhatsApp number and the town or area where you live;
  • a self-declaration about whether you have the Right to Work in the UK, and whether you hold a full UK driving licence;
  • the type of delivery opportunity that interests you, your delivery experience, when you could start and which days you are generally available;
  • your choices about the Privacy Notice, job update messages and the WhatsApp community, and the date and notice version you agreed to; and
  • if you arrived through a campaign link, a short label for where you came from (for example “facebook”).

We do not ask for passports or other ID, immigration documents, National Insurance numbers, dates of birth, your full home address, bank details or photographs of your driving licence. Checks of that kind, where they are needed, happen later and separately.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To review your interest and contact you about suitable opportunities. Our legal basis is legitimate interests: connecting people with work is what we do, and you have asked us to look at your details.
  • To send you job updates (optional). Only if you tick the box. Our legal basis is your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • To invite you to our WhatsApp community (optional). Only if you tick the box. Our legal basis is your consent. Members of a WhatsApp group may be able to see each other's phone numbers and profile information. WhatsApp is run by Meta and has its own privacy policy. Joining a group does not guarantee work and does not replace any recruitment checks.
  • To keep the service secure and free from spam. Our legal basis is legitimate interests. We use your connection details only briefly to limit abuse. We do not store them with your registration.

Who can see your information

  • LendHand team members who sign in to our private dashboard and need the information to do their job.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the data on our behalf and may only act on our instructions.
  • A hiring organisation, but only if we are putting you forward for a specific role. We will tell you who it is before we share your details.

We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

We delete registrations automatically {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days after they were last updated, unless you have been taken on for a role. If you are taken on, we will explain separately how long we keep your records. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or stop using it for particular purposes. You can also withdraw your consent for job updates or the WhatsApp community whenever you like. To do any of these, contact us at {{CONTACT}}.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Security

This site uses an encrypted connection. Registrations are stored in a private database that is not visible to the public, and only authorised team members with a password can open the dashboard.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Team members who sign in to the private dashboard receive one essential cookie to keep them signed in.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the version number above.
